# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Kriyan is for a technically capable owner who wants a personal productivity system they can inspect and operate across desktop, web, mobile, Convex Cloud, and one private VPS.

## Product Purpose

Kriyan combines planning and execution: tasks, reminders, calendar, notes, and a second brain keep the owner on track, while personal agents running on an owner-controlled computer or VPS do work through Convex. The public site must explain that system plainly and provide an honest path from architecture to installation, operation, updates, and recovery.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, capable, and candid. The product should feel like a carefully annotated system map: calm enough to read, specific enough to operate, and explicit about what has and has not been verified.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI interfaces built from neon gradients, glass cards, and vague autonomy claims.
- Chat-first framing that hides durable state, data authority, or operational responsibility.
- Infrastructure documentation that blurs source behavior, distribution readiness, and live proof.
- Dense terminal cosplay or editorial styling that makes practical guidance harder to scan.

## Design Principles

1. Explain the app to Convex to agent-node reactive loop before operational detail.
2. Show where each kind of data lives and which copy is authoritative.
3. Keep setup and recovery instructions scannable without concealing prerequisites.
4. Separate implemented, locally verified, deployed, released, and blocked states.
5. Make the same information usable on a phone at a server console and on a desktop during setup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and semantics. Support keyboard-only navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, zoom, phone and desktop layouts, and status communication that never relies on color alone.
