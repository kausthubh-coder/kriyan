What to try

  1. Tap a blue time in To plan to put that thing there.
  2. Hold a row (or just drag with a mouse) and drop it on a dashed free stretch. It snaps to 15 minutes.
  3. Tap a row or a block for its sheet: Start, Done, Move, Not today.
  4. Drag a study session to after lunch, then look at To plan: Kriyan learned from it.
  5. Tap the day name for the day menu, then Clear today.
  6. Press Add and type "Problem set 6 2h by fri", or tap an example. Tap a chip to change it; tap the blue time to place it.
  7. The right round button opens the week. Tap a blue time, drag a row onto a day, or drag a placed line to another day. Tap a day to open it.
  8. Menu has Goals and Settings.
