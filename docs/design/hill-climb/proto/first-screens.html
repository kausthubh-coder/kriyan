Why B. A block on the day always means you put it there, so the day never looks planned for you, which is the thing you ruled out. One list holds everything without a time: today's suggestions, new work suggested for another day, and things with no length yet. A needs a second strip for those. The same row and blue time is reused by Clear today and by new Moodle work, so there is one gesture to learn. What B costs is height: the day starts lower until the list empties, and A shows the shape of the morning sooner.
