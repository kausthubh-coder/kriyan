# Round 1: the day lays itself out

Change from round 0: Kriyan has its own planner. It needs no agent. Each morning (and whenever you ask) it lays out the free gaps of the day with what's waiting, as a suggested plan you accept with one tap. Everything else from round 0 stays.

A planner for one person (here: a university student). Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Month), desktop a left rail. Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI, through the same planner.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Where you make it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task | Something to tick off. Title only is required; date, time, length, deadline, repeat, reminders, space, goal, steps, note are optional. | Add (Task) |
| Event | A task you attend (class, meeting). Same editor, one switch. Fixed: the planner never moves it. | Add (Event) |
| Repeating task | A task with a rule ("every tue and thu 9am"). Each date is an occurrence. | Add, typed in the sentence |
| Deadline | A field on a task ("Due Fri 6 Nov"). | Task editor |
| Study session | A block the planner makes for a test: you say "about 6 h to study", it spreads sessions over the days before it. | The test's editor: "Plan study time" |
| Habit | "N times a week", with a length ("Gym, 45 min, 3 a week"). The planner gives it a slot on days you have room. Missing a day resets nothing. | Add (Habit), or Goals and habits |
| Routine | One block at a set time made of parts (Brush teeth 5 min, Gym 45 min Mon/Wed/Fri). A part can be a habit. | Goals and habits: Add a routine |
| Goal | A number with a target and date ($, kg) or a count of linked tasks. A goal can feed from habits and tasks, picked in its editor. | Goals and habits |
| Focus session | Timed work on a task, counted toward goals. | Focus |
| Space | Coloured container, nests (School > CSC 316). | Menu |
| Note, Steps | Markdown and a checklist inside a task or space. | Task editor |
| Proposal | A block the planner or an agent suggested, waiting for Accept. Drawn dashed. | The planner, agents |

All links (task → goal, habit → goal, routine part → habit, study session → test) can be set in the app.

## How you add things

One sentence in Add: "CSC 316 every tue and thu 10:15 for 1h15 #CSC316". It parses as you type; you pick Task, Event or Habit above it. A habit keeps its days if you typed them. No day goes to the Inbox. Lengths are never guessed; "for 30m" in the sentence, or the Fill step, gives one, and the planner only places things that have one.

## Today

- Big day name, week strip, a pull-down summary sentence.
- The day as time blocks from Wake up to Wind down, tinted by space, with dashed free gaps. Routines sit after Wake up or before Wind down.
- **Plan my day** (top of Today, and automatic the first time you open it each morning): the planner fills free gaps with, in order, what's due soonest, study sessions for the nearest test, habits still short of this week's target, then what's left from earlier. It leaves a gap of breathing room between blocks and never fills past your daily capacity. The result shows as dashed blocks with one bar: **Accept plan** / **Not now**. Ticking a dashed block also accepts it. Long-press one to swap it for something else.
- **To fit in**: whatever the planner couldn't place (no length, or no room), as a short checklist. Tap one to give it a length; drag it into a gap.
- **From earlier**: one quiet row; the planner already folds these into today's plan when there's room.
- Habits as rings; a habit with a slot today also shows as a block.
- Review card in the evening; "Close the day" moves what's left to tomorrow, where tomorrow's plan picks it up.

## The seven scenarios

1. **CSC 316, Tue and Thu 10:15–11:30.** Add, pick Event, type one sentence. Fixed block both days; the planner plans around it.
2. **Exam in 2 weeks, study for it.** Add the exam as an event. Open it, tap "Plan study time", say "about 6 h". The planner puts 45 to 90 min sessions into gaps over the two weeks, each saying "CSC 316 exam in 7 days". Missed ones roll into later gaps.
3. **Gym 3 times a week, feeding "lose 4 kg".** Add habit "Gym 45 min 3 times a week". The planner gives it slots on three days with room. Add goal "Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar" and pick Gym under "Fed by". Log weight with -0.5 kg.
4. **Morning routine.** Goals and habits, Add a routine: name, time (or "after Wake up"), parts with lengths. It's a block every morning with parts to tick.
5. **Save $1000 by 31 Dec.** Number goal with $ unit; log +$20 by hand. The card says "$84 a week for 9 weeks". Nothing is placed on a day.
6. **Mom's birthday, 14 Mar.** Task "Mom's birthday every year 14 mar". Reminder 09:00 that day by default.
7. **Buy stamps.** "Buy stamps 10m" goes to today's To fit in, and the planner can place it; without a length it waits in To fit in.

## Monday morning

You open Today. Classes are fixed blocks, the routine sits after Wake up, and the gaps are already filled with a dashed plan: Gym 45 min at 16:00, CSC 316 study 60 min at 13:30, "Lab report" (due Wed) 90 min at 19:00. You tap Accept plan. Or you tick the first block when you start it. To fit in holds the two things with no length.

## When the day slips

You missed the 13:30 study block. Today shows a quiet row: "1 block didn't happen. **Replan the rest**". One tap moves it, and anything after it that no longer fits, into later gaps or tomorrow, as a new dashed plan to accept. Nothing moves without that tap. "Close the day" in the evening still moves what's left to tomorrow.
