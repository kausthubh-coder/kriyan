# Round 5: a planner you can trust

Changes from round 4 (screens in `r5/`):

1. **When you can work.** The first run asks for your study windows on a week grid (mornings, afternoons, evenings, per day) and your daily work limit. Kriyan only places work inside those windows. Meals and travel are fixed repeats you can add in the same step.
2. **The limit and the reason are on screen.** Today shows "Work 3 h 15 of 4 h". Every heads-up says why ("Tue and Wed are full: classes and your 4 h limit") and offers time that's visibly free on the same screen.
3. **Fix one block, keep the rest.** Tap any block for a sheet: **Start**, **Done**, **Later today**, **Tomorrow**, **Keep here**. Only that block moves; Keep here pins it so the planner never moves it. "Undo plan" is still there for the whole morning plan.
4. **One word each.** Two time words: **at** (fixed: classes, exams) and **by** (a to-do's or goal's date). Counters read differently by kind: "Prep 1 of 5", "2 left this week", "1 h 30 left". Things you tick have a square box; things you attend (classes) have no box. Done on a prep block means this session; "Later" moves this session.
5. **The week is shown.** Month button opens the week: each day's blocks and a load bar against your limit. An agent's suggestion appears dashed with its name ("from Claude"), the only dashed thing in the app.

A planner for one person (here: a university student). Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Week), desktop a left rail. Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI, through the same planner.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Something that happens once. Title only is required. Optional: **by** (a date, maybe a time), **at** (a fixed time), a length, **prep** (hours of work before it), steps. | Buy stamps, 10m. Lab report, 3 h, by Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 6 h. |
| **Repeat** | Something that happens again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time: a class, a morning routine, lunch) or **flexible** (N times a week; Kriyan finds slots in your windows). Optional length and steps. | CSC 316, Tue and Thu 10:15 for 1h15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. Morning, 7:00: brush teeth, meditate, shower. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date: a number ($, kg) or "finish these". Shows what feeds it and whether that's on pace. | Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar. Save $1000 by 31 Dec. |

Holders, not things you do: **Space** (coloured, nests) and a **note** on anything.

On Today a block is **fixed** (a time you set; classes have no box), **placed** (Kriyan put it there; a small spark), or **pinned** (you said Keep here; a small pin). Free time is grey text. Dashed means only "an agent suggests this".

## First run (about 3 minutes)

1. Wake up and wind down (07:00, 23:00).
2. **When can you work?** A week grid of mornings, afternoons and evenings; tap to switch off what isn't usable (Fri evenings, Sun mornings). Then the daily limit: 2, 3, 4, 6 h.
3. **Your classes**: photo or paste of the timetable, shown as rows to tick or fix. Add lunch and travel as fixed repeats in one tap each.
4. **Anything due soon?** A few lines: "lab report 3h by wed", "csc 316 exam nov 23 9am prep 6h".
5. Today opens with the week planned inside your windows.

## How you add things

One sentence in Add, no kind picker; blue chips show what it understood. "Buy stamps 10m" is a to-do Kriyan places today; "Lab report 3h by wed" gets split into sessions; "CSC 316 exam nov 23 9am prep 6h" is fixed with prep spread before it; "Gym 45m 3 times a week" is a flexible repeat; "Save $1000 by dec 31" is a goal that asks "What gets you there?". With no length, **10m 30m 1h 2h** chips sit under the field; Add still saves without one; nothing is guessed.

## Today

- Day name and date.
- **Now card** (dark): what you're in, until when, with **Start / Done / Later**; "Next: …" underneath. Between blocks: "Free until 08:45. Next: Prep for CSC 316 exam". Also a live notification on the lock screen with the same buttons.
- One quiet line: "✦ Planned 3 blocks · Work 3 h 15 of 4 h · Undo plan".
- **Needs a length** (only when there are some): "Email TA 10m 30m 1h".
- The day: fixed, placed and pinned blocks, tinted by space; free time as grey text.
- **Heads-up** (only when true), with the reason and one fix.
- "2 not planned" at the end: what has no date and didn't fit this week.

## The seven scenarios

1. **CSC 316, Tue and Thu.** From the timetable photo. Fixed, no box.
2. **Exam in 2 weeks.** "CSC 316 exam nov 23 9am prep 6h". Five sessions land in your windows ("Prep 1 of 5"). A missed one is replanned; if prep can't fit, a heads-up says why.
3. **Gym 3 a week, feeding "lose 4 kg".** The goal offers "Gym 45m 3 a week"; three slots a week appear in afternoon windows ("2 left this week").
4. **Morning routine.** "Morning every day 7am" with steps. A block each morning.
5. **Save $1000 by 31 Dec.** The goal offers "Put aside $126 every Fri"; ticking it logs +$126.
6. **Mom's birthday.** "Mom's birthday every year mar 14". Reminder 09:00.
7. **Buy stamps.** "Buy stamps", tap 10m. Placed in a small gap today.
