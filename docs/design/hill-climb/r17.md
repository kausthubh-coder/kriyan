# Round 17: room every day, questions on the rows, one card question a week

A planner for one person (here: a university student). It plans their coursework into the free time around classes, and one card tells them what to do next. Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Week). Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI. Every screen in `r17/` is generated from one plan of the week (`r17/build.py`).

What it does:

1. **Room by design, and kept.** You set the most coursework a day can hold (4 h). Kriyan plans at most 3 h 30, so every day keeps at least 30 minutes of room, including after a fix. Work that doesn't fit in the room isn't squeezed in; it waits, and the toast says so ("Practice exam didn't fit in your room; it's waiting · Plan it anyway").
2. **A session that ended untouched asks on its own row.** "Did this happen? **Yes** · **Some** · **Move → 12:45**", in amber on that row of Today, until you answer. Kriyan doesn't assume it happened or didn't, and doesn't move it behind your back: it keeps the slot free ("12:45–14:15 kept free for it"). Nothing pops up; a grey line says "1 question on today".
3. **Ten minutes in, the lock-screen card notices.** "Lab report · session 1 of 2 hasn't started. Start · Move to 12:45."
4. **New work fits into room quietly.** A toast says where it went, with See and Undo.
5. **One card question a week, only when a deadline is at risk.** One headline, one line, **OK** (the default, which happens at the time it names) and **Other options**, details behind "See what moves". This week: the sick day, which you started by tapping "I'm sick" in the menu.

## The card

| Kind | When | Example | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **NEXT** | Free time, and something fits | "Free until 08:45 · Time for Email TA" | Start · Start exam prep early |
| **NOW** | A session's time | "CSC 316 exam prep · session 1 of 5 · until 09:45 · 7 min in", or "Lab report · session 1 of 2 hasn't started" | Done · +15m · Move to 12:45 (or Start · Move to 12:45) |
| **ASK** | A deadline is at risk | "Sick today? Kriyan clears today at 08:00." | OK · Other options |

When nothing needs doing, there's no card, only a grey line ("Lunch until 12:45 · then Lab report · session 1 of 2"). The card is also one live card on the lock screen, buttons visible without a long press.

## Words

- **Done** finishes a session. On the last session of a task the buttons are **Finished Lab report** · **Need more time**.
- **Move** moves one thing; the sheet says "Only this one moves".
- **Coursework** is what the daily limit counts; classes, meals, gym and errands don't.
- **Room** is how much more coursework a day could hold. **Free** is open time on the clock.
- **Kriyan** is the planner; **Claude** is your agent. Anything Claude changes says so, with Undo.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Once. Optional: **by** a date, **at** a time, a length, **prep** hours, steps. With a date, Kriyan plans it in sessions until you say it's finished; without, it waits and NEXT offers it in a free stretch. | Buy stamps. Lab report, 3 h, by Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 5 h. |
| **Repeat** | Again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time) or **flexible** (N a week; Kriyan finds slots). | CSC 316 lecture, Tue and Thu 10:15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. Wake-up routine, 7:00. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date, with what feeds it at a pace that gets there. | Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar (fed by Gym). Save $1000 by 31 Dec (Put aside $90, Fridays). |

On Today: a box for things you do, a clock for things you attend. Finished things fold into "Earlier today · N done".

## Setup (about 3 minutes)

Wake up and wind down; when you can work (Morning 07–12, Afternoon 12–17, Evening 17–23 per weekday) and the most coursework a day ("Kriyan plans about an hour less than this, so a slip or a new assignment fits without asking you"); classes and lunch from a timetable photo; anything due soon; allow the live card.
