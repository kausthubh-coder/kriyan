# Round 13: decisions with defaults, tasks that finish

Keeps round 12 (one card in four states on Today and the lock screen; every screen generated from one plan in `r13/build.py`; outcomes printed under Decide buttons; "session N of M"; earlier folds). Changes:

1. **Decide has a default.** The card says what Kriyan will do and when: "At 19:00 Kriyan moves one exam prep session from Wed to Sat to fit it, unless you choose." If you don't tap, it happens at that time with an Undo toast. Deciding is never a gate.
2. **Decide and Sunday are short.** A one-line headline (the thing), one line of what happens by default, and one short outcome under each button.
3. **A session done isn't the task done.** When the last planned session of a to-do ends, the check-in asks "Is Lab report finished?" Yes, finished · Need more → +1 h today 17:45 (and says if that goes past your usual day). A to-do with a due date but no length gets 1-hour sessions until you say it's finished; Kriyan never guesses how long it will take.
4. **A lost morning heals in one tap.** Unanswered check-ins gather into one card: "2 sessions had no check-in." **Neither happened: rebuild** moves them to the first free slots; "I did some of it" opens the per-session answers.
5. **Partial work says what it records:** "I worked 30m · 45m · 1h, and the rest goes to 12:45."
6. **Words.** The routine is "Wake-up routine" (Morning is only a work window). "Session" is only for split work: the gym sheet says "Only Gym moves". The Next card reads "Next at 12:45 · Lab report · session 1 of 2 · Lunch until then." The sheet has four big tiles (Start, Done, Later today → 16:45, Tomorrow → Tue 16:00) with Pin here and Edit as small links.
7. **One waiting thing at a time:** "Buy stamps fits now · Start" (then Call bank).
8. **Toasts don't make the plan sound wrong:** "Kriyan planned today around your classes". Undo appears after a change.

A planner for one person (here: a university student). Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Week). Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Once. Optional: **by** a date, **at** a time, a length, **prep** hours, steps. With a date, Kriyan plans it in sessions (1 h each when there's no length) until you say it's finished; without a date, it waits and the card offers it in a free stretch. | Buy stamps. Lab report, 3 h, by Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 5 h. |
| **Repeat** | Again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time) or **flexible** (N a week; Kriyan finds slots). | CSC 316 lecture, Tue and Thu 10:15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. Wake-up routine, 7:00. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date. Offers what feeds it, at a pace that gets there. | Read 3 books by 31 Dec: Read 40m, 5 evenings a week. |

On every screen: a box for things you do (ticked grey when done), a clock for things you attend (greyed once past). Dashed means only an agent's suggestion.
