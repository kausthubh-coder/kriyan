# Round 4: set up once, then just do the next thing

Changes from round 3 (screens in `r4/`):

1. **A 3-minute first run.** Wake up and wind down, hours of work a day, a photo or paste of the class timetable (turned into fixed repeats), and the next deadlines. It ends on a planned day.
2. **The plan is just the day.** Kriyan's own planner places blocks directly each morning; there is no Accept step and no dashed state for it. A small spark marks a block Kriyan placed, and the card says "Planned 4 blocks · Undo". Dashed blocks now mean only one thing: an agent's suggestion. Nothing moves after the morning unless you tap Replan.
3. **A Now card.** At the top of Today, all day: what you're in (or what's next), a Start button that times it, and what comes after. The same card is a live notification on the lock screen.
4. **Look-ahead.** When due work can't fit before its date, Today says so as soon as it becomes true, with one trade-off to accept ("Lab report: 3 h left, 1 h 30 free before Wed. Move Thursday's gym and it fits").
5. **Due, not On/By.** A person sets one of two kinds of time: **at** a fixed time (classes, exams, appointments) or **due** a date. Kriyan picks the day for everything else. "On Sat" is still possible for the rare thing that must be that day, but it isn't on the Add screen.
6. **Prep is part of the thing.** An exam or deadline has "Prep: 6 h". No second "Study for…" to-do. The sessions show under the exam's name.
7. **Routine steps are just steps.** A step doesn't count toward anything else, so nothing counts twice. Gym is its own flexible repeat.

A planner for one person (here: a university student). Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Month), desktop a left rail. Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI, through the same planner.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Something that happens once. Title only is required. Optional: **due** (a date, maybe a time), **at** (a fixed time), a length, **prep** (hours of work before it), steps. | Buy stamps, 10m. Lab report, 3 h, due Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 6 h. |
| **Repeat** | Something that happens again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time: a class, a morning routine) or **flexible** (N times a week; Kriyan finds the slots). Optional length and steps. | CSC 316, Tue and Thu 10:15 for 1h15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. Morning, 7:00: brush teeth, meditate, shower. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date: a number ($, kg) or "finish these". Shows what feeds it and whether that's on pace. | Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar. Save $1000 by 31 Dec. |

Holders, not things you do: **Space** (coloured, nests) and a **note** on anything.

On Today a block is one of: **fixed** (solid, a time you set), **placed** (solid with a small spark: Kriyan put it there this morning), or **suggested** (dashed: an agent's idea, waiting for you). Free time is plain grey text.

## First run (about 3 minutes)

1. "When do you usually wake up and wind down?" Two time wheels, 07:00 and 23:00.
2. "How many hours of work can a day hold?" 2, 4, 6, 8 (changeable later).
3. "Add your classes." Photo of the timetable, paste from the uni portal, or type them. Kriyan shows what it read as rows ("CSC 316, Tue Thu 10:15–11:30, Kord 1021") to tick or fix.
4. "Anything due soon?" Type a few lines: "lab report 3h due wed", "csc 316 exam nov 23 9am prep 6h".
5. Today opens with the week already planned around the classes.

## How you add things

One sentence in Add, no kind picker. The words decide:

- "Buy stamps 10m": a to-do; Kriyan places it in a small gap.
- "Lab report 3h due wed": a to-do split into sessions before Wednesday.
- "CSC 316 exam nov 23 9am prep 6h": a fixed to-do with 6 h of prep spread before it.
- "Gym 45m 3 times a week": a flexible repeat.
- "Save $1000 by dec 31": a goal, which then asks "What gets you there?" and offers "Put aside $126 every Fri".

Blue chips show what it understood. With no length, a row of **10m 30m 1h 2h** chips sits under the field; one tap. Add still saves without one; capture never blocks, and nothing is guessed.

## Today

- Day name, week strip.
- **Now card** (dark, at the top): "Now: Prep for CSC 316 exam, until 09:45" with **Start** (a timer) and **Done**; under it "Next: Lab report at 10:30". Between blocks it reads "Free until 08:45. Next: Prep for CSC 316 exam". On the lock screen as a live notification with the same two buttons.
- **The day**: fixed and placed blocks, solid, tinted by space. Placed ones carry a small spark. Free time is grey text. Each morning a quiet line: "Planned 3 blocks, 3 h 15 · Undo".
- **Heads-up** (only when true): "Lab report: 3 h left, 1 h 30 free before Wed. Move Thursday's gym to Fri and it fits." **Do that** / **Other options** (push the due date, trim to 2 h, let it go).
- **Needs a length** (only when there are some): "Email TA 10m 30m 1h". One tap places it.
- **Not planned** (one row: "2 not planned"): only what has no due date and didn't fit this week.

## The seven scenarios

1. **CSC 316, Tue and Thu.** Read from the timetable photo in the first run. Fixed.
2. **Exam in 2 weeks.** "CSC 316 exam nov 23 9am prep 6h". Six hours of sessions appear in gaps over two weeks, labelled "CSC 316 exam · prep 1 of 5". If a session is missed it's replanned; if prep can't fit, a heads-up says so early.
3. **Gym 3 a week, feeding "lose 4 kg".** "Weigh 72 kg by mar 1" asks "What gets you there?" and offers "Gym 45m 3 a week": tap Add it. Three gym blocks a week appear.
4. **Morning routine.** "Morning every day 7am", then steps (brush teeth, meditate, shower). One block each morning; tick steps or "All done".
5. **Save $1000 by 31 Dec.** The goal offers "Put aside $126 every Fri"; ticking it logs +$126.
6. **Mom's birthday.** "Mom's birthday every year mar 14". Reminder 09:00.
7. **Buy stamps.** "Buy stamps", tap 10m. Placed in a small gap today.

## Monday morning

Open Today at 07:40. The Now card says "Free until 08:45. Next: Study for CSC 316 exam". The day is already planned: morning routine done, exam prep at 08:45, lab report at 10:30, MA 241 at 14:30, gym at 16:00. One quiet line: "Planned 3 blocks, 3 h 15 · Undo". You don't decide anything. At 08:45 your phone shows the Now card; you tap Start.

## When the day slips

You skipped the 10:30 lab report session. At 13:50 the Now card says "Lab report didn't happen. **Replan the rest**". One tap moves it, and anything after it that no longer fits, into later gaps or tomorrow. If that pushes it past Wednesday, the heads-up appears with one trade-off.
