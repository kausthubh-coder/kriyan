# Round 6: every number checks out

Starts from round 5 (work windows, the limit on screen, fix one block, the week view). Changes (screens in `r6/`):

1. **The limit means one thing: study.** "Study 2 h 30 of 4 h" counts only to-dos and prep, the things with a "by" date. Classes, meals, routines and repeats like gym don't count; they show beside it ("+ gym 45m"). The first run says so.
2. **Every move says where it goes.** "Later → 12:45", "Tomorrow → Tue 16:00". Nothing moves to a place you can't see first.
3. **One Now card, everywhere.** Before a block starts: **Start** and **Later → time**. Once started: **Done** and **Later → time**, with the timer. The lock screen shows the same card and the same buttons.
4. **A heads-up shows the whole fix.** It lists where every remaining hour goes, all inside the limit, and what moves to make room. One tap: **Do that**.
5. **Replan the rest of today.** When two or more blocks didn't happen (or you tap "I lost the morning" in the menu), one card lists each move ("Prep 2 of 5 → 15:00") and **Apply** does them all; Undo puts them back.
6. **Windows have hours.** Morning 07–12, Afternoon 12–17, Evening 17–23.
7. **The week is readable.** Each day lists its blocks by name and "Study x of 4 h"; an agent's suggestion appears once, inline, dashed, with Accept / No.
8. **Dashed means agent, only.** Dividers are solid hairlines. A prep block reads "CSC 316 exam · prep 1 of 5"; its box ticks this session.

A planner for one person (here: a university student). Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Week), desktop a left rail. Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI, through the same planner.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Something that happens once. Title only is required. Optional: **by** (a date, maybe a time), **at** (a fixed time), a length, **prep** (hours of work before it), steps. | Buy stamps, 10m. Lab report, 3 h, by Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 5 h. |
| **Repeat** | Again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time: a class, a morning routine, lunch) or **flexible** (N times a week; Kriyan finds slots in your windows). Optional length and steps. | CSC 316, Tue and Thu 10:15 for 1h15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. Morning, 7:00: brush teeth, meditate, shower. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date: a number ($, kg) or "finish these". Shows what feeds it and whether that's on pace. | Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar. Save $1000 by 31 Dec. |

Holders: **Space** (coloured, nests) and a **note** on anything.

On Today: **fixed** blocks (classes, meals: a clock glyph, no box), **placed** blocks (Kriyan put them there: a small spark, a box to tick), **pinned** (you said Keep here: a pin). Free time is grey text. Dashed means only "an agent suggests this".

## First run (about 3 minutes)

1. Wake up and wind down (07:00, 23:00).
2. **When can you study?** A grid: Morning 07–12, Afternoon 12–17, Evening 17–23, for each weekday. Tap to switch one off. Then **Study a day, at most**: 2, 3, 4, 6 h. "Counts to-dos and exam prep. Classes, meals and gym don't count."
3. **Your classes** from a timetable photo or paste, as rows to tick. Lunch and travel as fixed repeats, one tap each.
4. **Anything due soon?** "lab report 3h by wed", "csc 316 exam nov 23 9am prep 5h", "eng 331 essay 4h by thu".
5. Today opens with the week planned inside your windows.

## How you add things

One sentence, no kind picker; blue chips show what it understood. With no length, **10m 30m 1h 2h** chips; Add saves without one too; nothing is guessed. A goal asks "What gets you there?" and offers a repeat.

## Today

- Day name and date.
- **Now card**: Start / Later → time before; Done / Later → time once started; "Next: …" under it. Same on the lock screen.
- One line: "✦ Planned 3 blocks · Study 2 h 30 of 4 h · + gym 45m · Undo plan".
- **Needs a length** (only when there are some).
- The day, with free time as grey text.
- **Heads-up** (only when true): the reason, every hour placed, what moves. Do that / Other options.
- "2 not planned" at the end.

## The seven scenarios

1. **CSC 316, Tue and Thu.** From the photo. Fixed.
2. **Exam in 2 weeks.** Prep 5 h becomes five 1-hour sessions in your windows, "CSC 316 exam · prep 1 of 5".
3. **Gym 3 a week, feeding "lose 4 kg".** The goal offers "Gym 45m, 3 a week"; Add it. Three afternoon slots a week, outside the study limit.
4. **Morning routine.** "Morning every day 7am" with steps.
5. **Save $1000 by 31 Dec.** The goal offers "Put aside $126 every Fri"; ticking it logs +$126.
6. **Mom's birthday.** "Mom's birthday every year mar 14". Reminder 09:00.
7. **Buy stamps.** "Buy stamps", tap 10m. Placed today.
