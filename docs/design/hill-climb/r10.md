# Round 10: nothing to learn but the day

Keeps round 9 (one card in four states on Today and the lock screen; the first free slot; undated to-dos fill gaps; Move → time; "· N of M"). The change: the planner's bookkeeping leaves the screen.

1. **No limit maths on screen.** Today and the week show the day, not "done · to go · limit". The daily limit is set once ("How much work a day, at most?") and the planner keeps to it silently. It appears only inside a Decide card, in plain words, when it changes a choice ("that's 1 h longer than you like to work on Wed").
2. **Two marks, everywhere.** A box for things you do (prep, to-dos, gym, errands, putting money aside); a clock for things you attend (classes, meals, dinner with Sam). Week and Today use the same marks. Done is a ticked box.
3. **The week is a timeline.** Each day lists its items in time order, classes in their slots, with the same names and marks as Today. Today's day is on top; it scrolls.
4. **Decide names its subject first.** "ENG 331 essay fits by Thu if one exam prep moves from Wed to Sat." The buttons are the outcomes. When it really can't fit, the options include working a bit longer, so neither is "be late": "Skip Practice exam (optional)" or "Keep it, work 1 h longer Thu and Fri".
5. **Same words for the same answer.** Check-in always offers Done · Did part · Move → time; a morning check-in offers the same three for all the blocks at once.
6. **Done says what's done.** On a split block: "Done with 1 of 5".
7. **Undo appears once, when there's something to undo:** a toast after a Decide choice ("Prep moved to Sat · Undo") or after Kriyan's morning plan the first time you open the day ("Kriyan planned today · Undo").

A planner for one person (here: a university student). Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Week). Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI, through the same planner.

## The card

| State | When | Example | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Next** | Free time | "Free until 08:45 · Email TA fits now. How long?" | 10m · 30m · 1h · Start exam prep early |
| **Now** | In a block | "CSC 316 exam prep · 1 of 5 · until 09:45 · 7 min in · After: Lab report · 1 of 2 at 10:30" | Done with 1 of 5 · Move → 12:45 |
| **Check-in** | A block ended with no tap | "Lab report · 1 of 2, 10:30–12:00. How did it go?" | Done · Did part · Move → 12:45 |
| **Decide** | Something won't fit | "ENG 331 essay fits by Thu if one exam prep moves from Wed to Sat." | Move prep to Sat · Keep Wed, work 1 h longer · See the times |

One card on Today and one live card on the lock screen, buttons visible without a long press. Reminders you set are the only other notifications.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Once. Optional: **by** a date, **at** a fixed time, a length, **prep** hours, steps. With a date or prep, Kriyan plans it; without, it waits and the card offers it in a free stretch. | Buy stamps, 10m. Lab report, 3 h, by Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 5 h. |
| **Repeat** | Again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time) or **flexible** (N a week; Kriyan finds slots). | CSC 316, Tue and Thu 10:15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. Morning, 7:00, with steps. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date. Offers what feeds it. | Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar. Save $1000 by 31 Dec. |

Holders: **Space** (coloured, nests) and a **note**. Dashed means only "an agent suggests this".

## First run (about 3 minutes)

Wake up and wind down; when you can work (Morning 07–12, Afternoon 12–17, Evening 17–23, per weekday); how much work a day, at most (4 h); classes from a timetable photo, and lunch; anything due soon; allow the live card. Today opens planned.
