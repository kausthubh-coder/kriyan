# Round 18: misses move forward; nothing asks you to look back

A planner for one person (here: a university student). It plans their coursework into the free time around classes, and one card tells them what to do next. Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Week). Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI. Every screen in `r18/` is generated from one plan of the week (`r18/build.py`).

What it does:

1. **Space by design.** You set the most coursework a day can hold (4 h). Kriyan plans at most 3 h 30, every day, including after a fix. Work that doesn't fit within that waits, and Kriyan says so ("Practice exam doesn't fit without going past 3 h 30 a day; it's waiting · Plan it anyway").
2. **A missed session moves forward, visibly.** If a session isn't started, it moves to the next free stretch that fits. Its old row stays greyed with "moved to 12:45"; the new row says "from 10:30". Nothing asks you to look back and say what happened.
3. **When it comes round, you can say you already did it.** At 12:45 the NOW card shows that session: **Start** · **I already did it** · **Skip today**. A lost morning works the same way, one session at a time, forward.
4. **Ten minutes in, the lock-screen card notices.** "CSC 316 lab report · session 1 of 2 hasn't started. Start · Move to 12:45."
5. **New work fits quietly.** A toast says where it went, with See and Undo.
6. **Kriyan asks one thing a week, only when a deadline is at risk.** One headline, one line, **OK** (the default, at the time it names) and **Other options**, details behind "See what moves". This week: the sick day, which you started by tapping "I'm sick" in the menu.

## The card

| Kind | When | Example | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **NEXT** | Free time, and something fits | "Free until 08:45 · Time for Email TA" | Start · Start exam prep early |
| **NOW** | A session's time | "CSC 316 exam prep · session 1 of 5 · until 09:45 · 7 min in"; "… hasn't started" (Start · Move to 12:45); "… moved from 10:30" (Start · I already did it · Skip today) | Done · +15m · Move to 12:45 |
| **ASK** | A deadline is at risk | "Sick today? Kriyan clears today at 08:00." | OK · Other options |

When nothing needs doing, there's no card, only a grey line ("Lunch until 12:45 · then Lab report · session 1 of 2"). The card is also one live card on the lock screen, buttons visible without a long press.

## Words

- **Done** finishes a session. On the last session of a task the buttons are **Finished Lab report** · **Need more time**.
- **Move** moves one thing; the sheet says "Only this one moves".
- **Coursework** is what the daily limit counts; classes, meals, gym and errands don't.
- **Free** means open time on the clock. The daily amount is said plainly: "2 h 30 of coursework planned today", "3 h 30 of 4 h".
- **Kriyan** is the planner; **Claude** is your agent. Anything Claude changes says so, with Undo.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Once. Optional: **by** a date, **at** a time, a length, **prep** hours, steps. With a date, Kriyan plans it in sessions until you say it's finished; without, it waits and NEXT offers it in a free stretch. | Buy stamps. CSC 316 lab report, 3 h, by Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 5 h. |
| **Repeat** | Again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time) or **flexible** (N a week; Kriyan finds slots). | CSC 316 lecture, Tue and Thu 10:15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. Wake-up routine, 7:00. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date, with what feeds it at a pace that gets there. | Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar (fed by Gym). Save $1000 by 31 Dec (Put aside $90, Fridays). |

On Today: a box for things you do, a clock for things you attend. Finished things fold into "Earlier today · N done".

## Setup (about 3 minutes)

Wake up and wind down; when you can work (Morning 07–12, Afternoon 12–17, Evening 17–23 per weekday) and the most coursework a day ("Kriyan plans about an hour less than this, so a slip or a new assignment fits without asking you"); classes and lunch from a timetable photo; anything due soon; allow the live card.
