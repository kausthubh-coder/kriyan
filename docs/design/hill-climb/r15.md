# Round 15: less to read on a bad day

A planner for one person (here: a university student). It plans their coursework into the free time around classes, and one card tells them what to do next. Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Week). Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI. Every screen in `r15/` is generated from one plan of the week (`r15/build.py`).

Changes from rounds 12 to 14: three card kinds instead of six; every question is one sentence plus what happens by default; the sick day's default does the whole job, including the extension email; the week fits on one screen; one word each for done, move and not now.

## The card: three kinds

One card at the top of Today and one live card on the lock screen (an iOS Live Activity, an Android ongoing notification), buttons visible without a long press. Reminders you set are the only other notifications.

| Kind | When | Example | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **NEXT** | Free time | "Free until 08:45 · Email TA fits now" | Start · Start exam prep early |
| **NOW** | In a session | "CSC 316 exam prep · session 1 of 5 · until 09:45 · 7 min in" | Done · +15m · Stop, finish at 12:45 |
| **ASK** | Kriyan needs an answer | One sentence, then what Kriyan does by default and when. | Do that now · one alternative. Details behind "See what moves". |

Rules for ASK:
- One sentence for the news, one for the default and its time. If you don't answer, the default happens at that time, with an Undo toast. Nothing waits on you.
- Never two ASK cards at once. While an ASK is up, a grey line above it says what's next ("Next · Dinner with Sam at 19:00").
- On the last session of a task, NOW's buttons are "Done, finished" and "Done, need more", so finishing never needs a separate question.

Starting the next thing counts the one before as done, and the NOW card says so in a line: "Email TA counted as done when you started this. Not done?" An ASK appears only when nothing was started since a session began.

## Words

- Done means this session. Finished means the whole task.
- Move means one thing moves; the sheet says "Only this one moves". "Not now" on an offer. "Off today" for classes on a sick day.
- Work means coursework: the daily limit (4 h) counts only that. Classes, meals, gym and errands don't count, and the week view lists only coursework.
- Kriyan is the planner; Claude is your agent. Anything Claude changes says so, with Undo.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Once. Optional: **by** a date, **at** a time, a length, **prep** hours, steps. With a date, Kriyan plans it in sessions (1 h each when there's no length) until you say it's finished; without, it waits and NEXT offers it in a free stretch. | Buy stamps. Lab report, 3 h, by Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 5 h. |
| **Repeat** | Again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time) or **flexible** (N a week; Kriyan finds slots). | CSC 316 lecture, Tue and Thu 10:15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. Wake-up routine, 7:00. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date. Offers what feeds it, at a pace that gets there. | Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar (fed by Gym). Read 3 books by 31 Dec (Read 40m, 5 evenings a week). |

On Today: a box for things you do, a clock for things you attend. Finished things fold into "Earlier today · N done".

## Setup (about 3 minutes)

Wake up and wind down; when you can work (Morning 07–12, Afternoon 12–17, Evening 17–23 per weekday) and how much a day at most; classes and lunch from a timetable photo; anything due soon; allow the live card.
