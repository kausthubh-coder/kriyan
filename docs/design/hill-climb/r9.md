# Round 9: one card, plain choices

Keeps round 8's structure (one card, four states, on Today and as one live lock-screen card; Check-in asks instead of assuming). Changes:

1. **The planner takes the obvious slot.** Moving a block goes to the next free stretch inside your windows that fits it (the lab report → 12:45, right after lunch), never a later one when an earlier one is open.
2. **Dated things are planned; undated things fill gaps.** To-dos with a "by" date or prep get placed in the plan. To-dos with no date wait in Not planned, and the Next card offers them when a free stretch fits ("Buy stamps 10m and Call bank 15m fit now").
3. **Decide is two lines and two buttons.** The headline is the outcome ("Essay fits by Thu if exam prep moves Wed → Sat"). Each button says its own outcome ("Move prep to Sat" / "Keep Wed, go 1 h over"). The hours are under "See the times", and once you choose, the new blocks appear in the day list.
4. **One verb for moving: Move → time.** On the card, the lock screen and the block sheet: "Move → 12:45", "Move → Tue 16:00". No Later, Not yet or Tomorrow.
5. **One pattern for split work: "· N of M".** "CSC 316 exam prep · 1 of 5", "Lab report · 1 of 2", "ENG 331 essay · 1 of 3". Numbers follow the date order, so a moved session is renumbered. The button is just Done.
6. **One word for what follows: After.** "After: Lab report · 1 of 2 at 12:45". No Next-inside-Now, no Then bar.
7. **The limit says it's a limit, and counts planned work only:** "1 h done · 1 h 30 to go · limit 4 h". It counts exam prep and to-dos with a by date, the things the planner places. Quick undated to-dos that fill gaps (an email, stamps), classes, meals, gym and money don't count; the week marks them apart.
8. **Clock means you attend** (classes, meals, dinner with Sam). A routine you do at a fixed time (Morning) has a box.

A planner for one person (here: a university student). Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Week), desktop a left rail. Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI, through the same planner.

## The card

One card at the top of Today and as one live card on the lock screen (iOS Live Activity, Android ongoing notification), buttons visible without a long press. It changes in place. Reminders you set are the only other notifications.

| State | When | Example | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Next** | Free time | "Free until 08:45 · Email TA fits now. How long?" | 10m · 30m · 1h · Start exam prep early |
| **Now** | In a block | "CSC 316 exam prep · 1 of 5 · until 09:45 · 7 min in · After: Lab report · 1 of 2 at 10:30" | Done · Move → 12:45 |
| **Check-in** | A block ended with no tap | "Lab report · 1 of 2, 10:30–12:00. How did it go?" | Done · Did part · Move → 12:45 |
| **Decide** | Something won't fit | "Essay fits by Thu if exam prep moves Wed → Sat." | Move prep to Sat · Keep Wed, go 1 h over · See the times |

"Did part" asks how much (15m · 30m · 45m · 1h) and plans only the rest. A Decide card shows "After this: free until 19:00" as its last line.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Something that happens once. Title only is required. Optional: **by** (a date, maybe a time), **at** (a fixed time), a length, **prep** (hours before it), steps. With a "by" date or prep it's planned; without, it waits in Not planned and fills gaps. | Buy stamps, 10m. Lab report, 3 h, by Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 5 h. |
| **Repeat** | Again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time) or **flexible** (N times a week; Kriyan finds slots). Optional length and steps. | CSC 316, Tue and Thu 10:15 for 1h15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. Morning, 7:00, with steps. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date. Offers what feeds it. | Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar. Save $1000 by 31 Dec. |

Holders: **Space** (coloured, nests; legend in the week) and a **note** on anything. On Today: a clock means you attend it; a box means you do it. Dashed means only "an agent suggests this".

## First run (about 3 minutes)

Wake up and wind down; when you can work (Morning 07–12, Afternoon 12–17, Evening 17–23 per weekday) and a limit (4 h a day of planned work: exam prep and to-dos with a by date; not quick errands, classes, meals, gym or money); classes from a timetable photo, and lunch; anything due soon; allow the live card. Today opens planned.

## How you add things

One sentence, no kind picker; blue chips show what it understood. With no length, **10m 30m 1h 2h** chips; Add saves without one too. A goal asks "What gets you there?" and offers a repeat: Add it · Change · Not now.
