# Round 19: one number, one rule for misses, nothing to read on a good day

A planner for one person (here: a university student). It plans their coursework into the free time around classes, and one card tells them what to do next. Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Week). Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI. Every screen in `r19/` is generated from one plan of the week (`r19/build.py`).

What it does:

1. **One number.** You set the most coursework a day can hold (4 h). That's the only number you ever see. Kriyan quietly keeps part of each day free so a slip or new work always has somewhere to go; it never shows a second limit.
2. **One rule for a session you haven't started: start it, or it moves at a stated time.** Ten minutes in, the lock-screen card says **NOT STARTED**, with **Start** · **Done already**, and "Moves to 12:45 at 10:50 unless you start." Nothing else to learn.
3. **Misses fold away.** Today shows one grey line at the top ("↪ 1 moved forward: CSC 316 lab report, 10:30 → 12:45 ›"), not ghost rows. The moved session sits at its new time tagged "from 10:30", and when it comes round the card offers **Start** · **Done already** · **Tomorrow**.
4. **New work fits quietly.** A toast says where it went ("No day goes past your 4 h"), with See and Undo. What doesn't fit without a long day waits, and says so.
5. **Kriyan asks only when a deadline is at risk.** One headline, one line, **OK** and **Other options**, details behind a tap. This week: once, after you told it you were sick.

## The card

| Kind | When | Example | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **NEXT** | Free time, and something fits | "Free until 08:45 · Time for Email TA"; "Buy stamps fits before dinner" | Start · Start exam prep early |
| **NOW** | You're in a session | "CSC 316 exam prep · session 1 of 5 · until 09:45 · 7 min in" | Done · +15m |
| **NOT STARTED** | A session's time passed 10 minutes ago | "CSC 316 lab report · session 1 of 2 · due at 10:30. Moves to 12:45 at 10:50 unless you start." | Start · Done already |
| **ASK** | A deadline is at risk | "Sick today? Kriyan clears today at 08:00." | OK · Other options |

When nothing needs doing, there's no card, only a grey line ("Lunch until 12:45 · then Lab report · session 1 of 2"). The card is also one live card on the lock screen, buttons visible without a long press.

## Words

- **Done** finishes a session (**Done already** if you did it without starting the timer). On the last session of a task: **Done, all finished** · **Done, more needed**.
- **Move** moves one thing; the sheet says "Only this one moves".
- **Coursework** is what the daily limit counts; classes, meals, gym and errands don't.
- **Free** means open time on the clock. The week shows each day's coursework plainly ("3 h 30 of coursework").
- **Kriyan** is the planner; **Claude** is your agent. Anything Claude changes says so, with Undo.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Once. Optional: **by** a date, **at** a time, a length, **prep** hours, steps. With a date, Kriyan plans it in sessions until you say it's finished; without, it waits and NEXT offers it in a free stretch. | Buy stamps. CSC 316 lab report, 3 h, by Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 5 h. |
| **Repeat** | Again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time) or **flexible** (N a week; Kriyan finds slots). | CSC 316 lecture, Tue and Thu 10:15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. Wake-up routine, 7:00. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date, with what feeds it at a pace that gets there. | Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar (fed by Gym). Save $1000 by 31 Dec (Put aside $90, Fridays). |

On Today: a box for things you do, a clock for things you attend. Finished things fold into "Earlier today · N done".

## Setup (about 3 minutes)

Step 4 shows what Moodle (connected through Claude, your agent) found due soon, to tick or add to; step 5 allows the live card.

Wake up and wind down; when you can work (Morning 07–12, Afternoon 12–17, Evening 17–23 per weekday) and the most coursework a day ("Kriyan plans about an hour less than this, so a slip or a new assignment fits without asking you"); classes and lunch from a timetable photo; anything due soon; allow the live card.
