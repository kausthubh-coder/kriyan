# Round 12: one plan, every screen

Keeps round 11 (one card in four states on Today and the lock screen; every button an outcome; no limit maths on screen; two marks; the Sunday look back and ahead; "Kriyan noticed"). Every screen in `r12/` is generated from one plan of the week (`r12/build.py`), so the story can't contradict itself. Changes:

1. **"Still working +15m"** on the Now card extends the session and pushes what follows, saying so if it touches a deadline.
2. **Decide prints each outcome under its button.** "Move prep to Sat: Essay tonight 20:30, Tue 19:00, Wed 20:00. Prep session 3 → Sat 13:00." No need to open the times to choose.
3. **Decide says where it came from:** "New from Moodle".
4. **Check-in partial is chips, not a button:** "Did some? 30m · 45m · 1h, the rest goes to 12:45". Done and Move → 12:45 stay the two buttons.
5. **One word, session.** Split work is "session N of M"; the Sunday card counts sessions. Classes are named for what they are ("CSC 316 lecture", "ENG 331 seminar") so they can't be mixed up with the prep for that course.
6. **Today folds the past.** Finished things collapse into "Earlier today · 5 done"; the day shows what's left.
7. **Prep is spread.** CSC 316 exam (Mon 23): sessions on Mon 9, Tue 10, Sat 14, Thu 19, Sun 22.
8. **The setup is shown** (when you can work; classes and lunch from a timetable photo).

A planner for one person (here: a university student). Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Week). Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI.

## The card

| State | When | Example | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Next** | Free time | "Free until 08:45 · Email TA fits now" | Start · Start exam prep early |
| **Now** | In a session | "CSC 316 exam prep · session 1 of 5 · until 09:45 · 7 min in" | Done with session 1 · Still working +15m · Move → 12:45 |
| **Check-in** | A session ended with no tap | "Lab report · session 1 of 2, 10:30–12:00. How did it go?" | Done · Move → 12:45 · Did some? 30m 45m 1h |
| **Decide** | Something new won't fit | "New from Moodle: ENG 331 essay (4 h, by Thu) fits if one exam prep session moves from Wed to Sat." | Each choice with its outcome printed under it |

One card on Today, one live card on the lock screen; buttons visible without a long press. Reminders you set are the only other notifications. Undo appears as a toast after Kriyan or you change the plan.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Once. Optional: **by** a date, **at** a time, a length, **prep** hours, steps. With a date or prep, Kriyan plans it in sessions; without, it waits and the card offers it in a free stretch. | Buy stamps. Lab report, 3 h, by Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 5 h. |
| **Repeat** | Again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time) or **flexible** (N a week; Kriyan finds slots). | CSC 316 lecture, Tue and Thu 10:15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. Morning, 7:00. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date. Offers what feeds it. | Finish 6 books by 31 Dec. Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar. |

On every screen: a box for things you do (ticked grey when done), a clock for things you attend (greyed once past). Dashed means only an agent's suggestion.
