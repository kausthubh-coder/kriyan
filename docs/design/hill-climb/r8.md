# Round 8: one card

Starts from round 6, keeping round 7's lock-screen healing, the overload recommendation and the action in free time. The structural change: every prompt Kriyan makes is **one card**, in one place.

## The card

The card sits at the top of Today and, on the phone, is one live card on the lock screen (an iOS Live Activity, an Android ongoing notification). It's one card, not a stream of pings; it changes in place, and its buttons are visible without a long press. The day list is always under it on Today. It's in one of four states:

| State | When | What it says | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Next** | Free time | "Free until 08:45" and the one thing that fits, or "Nothing that fits; free time is free" | Do it now (with a length if needed) · Start the next one early |
| **Now** | In a block | "CSC 316 exam · prep 1 of 5 · until 09:45 · 7 min in" | Session done · Later → 12:45 |
| **Check-in** | A block ended without a tap | "Lab report · 10:30–12:00. How did it go?" | Done · Did part · Not yet → 17:00 |
| **Decide** | Something won't fit | The reason, the recommended fix with every hour placed, and the other option in plain words | Do that · Instead: … |

Rules:
- One state at a time. A Decide card goes on top of Next or Now and shows "Then: Free until 19:00" under it, so what's next never disappears.
- Kriyan never assumes a block didn't happen; the Check-in asks. "Did part" asks how much (15m · 30m · 45m · 1h) and plans only what's left.
- Reminders you set (a class in 10 minutes, a birthday) are the only other notifications.

## Words and names

- A thing has one name everywhere, and split work counts its parts: **"Lab report · part 1 of 2"**, **"CSC 316 exam · prep 2 of 5"**, **"ENG 331 essay · part 1 of 3"**. Today, the card, the lock screen and the week use the same string.
- Work is counted as **done · to go · of 4 h** ("1 h 10 done · 1 h 30 to go · of 4 h"). It counts to-dos and prep; not classes, meals, gym or money.
- Plain verbs: "Do that", "Instead: …", "Not yet → 17:00". A block's sheet says "Keep here: Kriyan won't move it". The morning line says "Kriyan planned today at 06:00 · Undo".

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Something that happens once. Title only is required. Optional: **by** (a date, maybe a time), **at** (a fixed time), a length, **prep** (hours before it), steps. | Buy stamps, 10m. Lab report, 3 h, by Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 5 h. |
| **Repeat** | Again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time: a class, a routine, lunch) or **flexible** (N times a week; Kriyan finds slots). Optional length and steps. | CSC 316, Tue and Thu 10:15 for 1h15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date. Shows what feeds it and whether that's on pace. | Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar. Save $1000 by 31 Dec. |

Holders: **Space** (coloured, nests; legend in the week) and a **note** on anything.

On Today: a clock means fixed (you attend; nothing to tick); a box means something you do. Gym has a box everywhere, the week included. Dashed means only "an agent suggests this".

## First run (about 3 minutes)

1. Wake up and wind down. 2. When can you work (Morning 07–12, Afternoon 12–17, Evening 17–23 per weekday) and work a day at most (4 h). 3. Classes from a timetable photo; lunch. 4. Anything due soon. 5. Allow the live card on the lock screen. Today opens planned.

## How you add things

One sentence, no kind picker; blue chips show what it understood. With no length, **10m 30m 1h 2h** chips; Add saves without one too, and the Next card offers it in a free stretch with the same chips. A goal asks "What gets you there?" and offers a repeat.
