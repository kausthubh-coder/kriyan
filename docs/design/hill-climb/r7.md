# Round 7: the plan heals itself from the lock screen

Starts from round 6. Changes (screens in `r7/`):

1. **The Now card always has an action.** In a free stretch it offers what fits: "Free until 08:45. Email TA fits now: 10m · 30m" (tap a length and it starts) or "Start prep early". Nothing is guessed: the length is your tap.
2. **Healing works from the lock screen.** The slip card ("Lab report didn't happen · Move to 17:00"), the replan card ("2 blocks didn't happen · Apply") and the time's-up card ("Prep 1 of 5: time's up · Done · 15 more min · Not finished") arrive as notifications with their buttons. You never have to open Today to keep the plan whole.
3. **When it really can't fit, one recommendation.** "Too much before Fri: 2 h more than your days hold. Recommended: skip Practice exam (optional) and do Problem set 6 on Sat, 1 day after it's due." Or the other trade-off, named just as plainly: "Go 1 h over on Thu and Fri." Each option names what slips and which deadline it touches.
4. **Fewer markers.** Two only: a clock for fixed things you attend (no box), a box for things you do. Dashed still means only "an agent suggests this". Who placed a block, and Keep here, live in the block's sheet, not as icons.
5. **The same name everywhere.** "CSC 316 exam · prep 1 of 5" on Today, the lock screen and the week. Every block shows its time range.
6. **"Work" is the limit.** "Work 2 h 30 planned, of 4 h". It counts to-dos and prep (essays, labs, emails); not classes, meals or repeats like gym. Gym isn't shown beside it.
7. **The week has a legend and shows classes,** greyed, so a day's real load is visible. Each day: "4 h planned, of 4 h".
8. **Undo says what it undoes:** "Undo this morning's plan" puts placed blocks back to Not planned; nothing you ticked changes.

A planner for one person (here: a university student). Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Week), desktop a left rail. Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI, through the same planner.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Something that happens once. Title only is required. Optional: **by** (a date, maybe a time), **at** (a fixed time), a length, **prep** (hours of work before it), steps. | Buy stamps, 10m. Lab report, 3 h, by Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 5 h. |
| **Repeat** | Again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time: a class, a morning routine, lunch) or **flexible** (N times a week; Kriyan finds slots in your windows). Optional length and steps. | CSC 316, Tue and Thu 10:15 for 1h15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. Morning, 7:00: brush teeth, meditate, shower. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date: a number ($, kg) or "finish these". Shows what feeds it and whether that's on pace. | Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar. Save $1000 by 31 Dec. |

Holders: **Space** (coloured, nests; its name shows in grey after a title, and the week has a legend) and a **note** on anything.

On Today: a clock means fixed (you attend it; nothing to tick); a box means something you do (tick it, or Done on the Now card). Free time is grey text. Dashed means only "an agent suggests this".

## First run (about 3 minutes)

1. Wake up and wind down (07:00, 23:00).
2. **When can you work?** Morning 07–12, Afternoon 12–17, Evening 17–23 for each weekday; tap to switch one off. **Work a day, at most**: 2, 3, 4, 6 h. "Counts to-dos and prep, like essays, labs and emails. Not classes, meals or gym."
3. **Your classes** from a timetable photo or paste. Lunch and travel as fixed repeats.
4. **Anything due soon?** "lab report 3h by wed", "csc 316 exam nov 23 9am prep 5h", "eng 331 essay 4h by thu".
5. **Let Kriyan reach your lock screen** (one switch): so a slipped block can be fixed from there.
6. Today opens with the week planned.

## How you add things

One sentence, no kind picker; blue chips show what it understood. With no length, **10m 30m 1h 2h** chips; Add saves without one too, and the Now card offers it in a free stretch later. A goal asks "What gets you there?" and offers a repeat.

## Today

- Day name and date.
- **Now card**, always with an action. Free: "Free until 08:45" plus what fits (a quick one, or start the next block early). Before a block: Start / Later → time. In a block: Done / Later → time, with the timer. At the end of a block: Done / 15 more min / Not finished (adds a session).
- One line: "Work 2 h 30 planned, of 4 h · Undo this morning's plan".
- The day: every block with its time range; free time as grey text.
- **Heads-up** (only when true): the reason, where every hour goes, what moves, or, when it can't fit, one recommendation naming what slips.
- "2 not planned" at the end.

## The seven scenarios

1. **CSC 316, Tue and Thu.** From the photo. Fixed, clock.
2. **Exam in 2 weeks.** Prep 5 h becomes five 1-hour sessions in your windows, "CSC 316 exam · prep 1 of 5".
3. **Gym 3 a week, feeding "lose 4 kg".** The goal offers "Gym 45m, 3 a week"; Add it. Mon, Wed, Fri afternoons, outside the work limit.
4. **Morning routine.** "Morning every day 7am" with steps.
5. **Save $1000 by 31 Dec.** The goal offers "Put aside $126 every Fri"; ticking it logs +$126.
6. **Mom's birthday.** "Mom's birthday every year mar 14". Reminder 09:00.
7. **Buy stamps.** "Buy stamps", tap 10m. Placed today.
