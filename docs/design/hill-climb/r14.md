# Round 14: a bad week, handled

Builds on round 13 (one card in four states on Today and the lock screen; every screen generated from one plan in `r14/build.py`; Decide with a default; "Is it finished?" after the last session; one-tap rebuild of a lost morning). Changes:

1. **A sick day.** Menu → "I'm sick today". Kriyan clears the day and a Decide card lists where each piece of today's work goes, marked on time or late: essay session 3 → Thu 16:00 (on time), Problem set 5 → Fri 13:15 (on time), Lab report session 2 → Fri 08:00 (2 days late), gym skipped (2 of 3 this week). Default at 08:00: Move them. Second choice: "Draft an extension email to your TA", which you read before it's sent.
2. **New work into a full week.** Thu: Problem set 6 (4 h, by Mon) and Practice exam (2 h, optional) arrive; Thu and Fri are full since the sick day. Default at 10:00: skip Practice exam, Problem set 6 Sat 14:30–17:00 and Sun 15:00–16:30, on time. Or keep both: Sunday becomes a 4 h 30 day.
3. **An honest Sunday.** "A sick Wednesday cost one deadline. Lab report went in Fri, 2 days late, with your TA's OK. Everything else on time; Problem set 6 is done a day early."
4. **Zero prompts on a good day.** Starting the next thing ticks the one before it (a toast says so, with Undo). A Check-in appears only when nothing was started since a session began; the card says that's why it's asking.
5. **What's next stays on top.** Above any Decide card on Today, a black strip: "Now · free until 19:00 · then Dinner with Sam".
6. **Labels say the job:** NEXT, NOW, CHECK-IN (how did a session go), MISSED (gathered sessions with no answer), FINISHED? (is the task done), DECIDE.
7. **"Done with session 1"** on every check-in of split work, so Done never sounds like the whole task.
8. **The week shows load:** each day's header says how much work it holds ("4 h of work"), each row its length, and an agent's suggestion has Accept and No on the row.
9. **Words:** "planned by Kriyan this morning", "helps Weigh 72 kg", "Only this one moves".

A planner for one person (here: a university student). Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Week). Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI.

## The card

| State | When | Example | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Next** | Free time | "Free until 08:45 · Email TA fits now" | Start · Start exam prep early |
| **Now** | In a session | "CSC 316 exam prep · session 1 of 5 · until 09:45 · 7 min in" | Done with session 1 · Still working +15m · Move → 12:45 |
| **Check-in** | A session ended with no tap | "Lab report · session 1 of 2, 10:30–12:00. How did it go?" | Done · Move → 12:45 · I worked 30m 45m 1h |
| **Decide** | Something new won't fit | "ENG 331 essay, 4 h, by Thu. At 19:00 Kriyan moves one exam prep session from Wed to Sat, unless you choose." | Move prep to Sat · Keep Wed as it is |

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Once. Optional: **by** a date, **at** a time, a length, **prep** hours, steps. With a date, Kriyan plans it in sessions (1 h each when there's no length) until you say it's finished; without a date, it waits and the card offers it in a free stretch. | Buy stamps. Lab report, 3 h, by Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 5 h. |
| **Repeat** | Again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time) or **flexible** (N a week; Kriyan finds slots). | CSC 316 lecture, Tue and Thu 10:15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. Wake-up routine, 7:00. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date. Offers what feeds it, at a pace that gets there. | Read 3 books by 31 Dec: Read 40m, 5 evenings a week. |

On every screen: a box for things you do (ticked grey when done), a clock for things you attend (greyed once past). Dashed means only an agent's suggestion.
