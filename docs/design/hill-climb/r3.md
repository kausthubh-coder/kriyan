# Round 3: everything gets a length, so everything gets placed

Change from round 2: a missing length no longer leaves things stuck. Add offers one-tap length chips, and the morning plan asks for any missing lengths inline, one tap each. Kriyan still never guesses a length; the person taps it. The holding place is renamed from Waiting to **Not planned** ("Waiting" read as "waiting on someone"). A to-do's day and due date read as **On Wed** and **By Wed** everywhere. Everything else is round 2: three kinds (to-do, repeat, goal) and the built-in planner.

A planner for one person (here: a university student). Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Month), desktop a left rail. Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI, through the same planner.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Something that happens once. Title only is required. Optional: a day (**On Wed**: do it then), a due date (**By Wed**: done by then), a time, a length, steps. | Buy stamps. Lab report, 3 h, due Wed. CSC 316 exam, Mon 23 Nov 9:00. |
| **Repeat** | Something that happens again and again. Either **fixed** (set days and time: a class) or **flexible** (N times a week: the planner finds the slots). Optional length and steps. | CSC 316, Tue and Thu 10:15 for 1h15 (fixed). Gym, 45 min, 3 a week (flexible). Morning, 7:00: brush teeth 5, meditate 10, shower 10 (fixed, with steps). |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date: a number ($, kg) or "finish these". It shows what feeds it and whether that's enough. | Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar. Save $1000 by 31 Dec. |

Holders, which aren't things you do: **Space** (coloured, nests: School > CSC 316) and a **note** on anything.

How the old words map:

| Old kind | Now |
| --- | --- |
| Task | To-do |
| Event | A to-do or repeat with a fixed time. The planner never moves anything with a fixed time; it just shows it. Nothing to switch. |
| Repeating task | Fixed repeat |
| Habit | Flexible repeat ("3 a week"), with a ring and "1 of 3 this week". Missing a day resets nothing. |
| Routine | A fixed repeat with steps. Ticking a step that is itself a repeat ("Gym" inside Morning) counts for both. |
| Deadline | A to-do's due date, shown as "By Wed". A day shows as "On Wed". |
| Study session | Not a kind. A to-do with a length and a due date gets split by the planner into sessions before it ("Lab report, 3 h, due Wed" becomes two 90-min blocks). "Study for CSC 316 exam, 6 h, due Sun" works the same way. |
| Focus session | Not a kind. Every block has Start, which times it. |
| Proposal | "Suggested": a dashed block from the planner or an agent. Free time is plain text in the gap ("1 h 45 free"), never dashed. |

## How you add things

One sentence in Add, no kind picker. The words decide:

- "Buy stamps" is a to-do with no day: it goes to Not planned.
- "Lab report 3h due wed" is a to-do the planner will split before Wednesday.
- "CSC 316 every tue and thu 10:15 for 1h15" is a fixed repeat.
- "Gym 45m 3 times a week" is a flexible repeat.
- "Save $1000 by dec 31" is a goal.

The chips under the field show what it understood, in blue ("Repeat · 3 a week · 45 min", "By Wed"); tap one to change it.

**Length in one tap.** If the sentence has no length, a row of quiet chips sits under it: **10m 30m 1h 2h**. Tap one and Add saves with it. Press Add or Enter without one and it saves anyway; capture never blocks. Kriyan never picks a length for you.

Making a goal asks one follow-up: **"What gets you there?"** It offers a repeat or to-do to feed it, filled in from the goal ("Gym 45m 3 a week", "Put aside $84 every Fri"), which you accept, change or skip.

## Today

- Big day name, week strip, a pull-down summary sentence.
- The day as blocks from Wake up to Wind down, tinted by space. Fixed things are solid, suggested ones dashed, free gaps plain text.
- **Plan my day** (automatic the first time you open Today each morning): the planner fills free gaps with, in order, what's due soonest (split into sessions if long), flexible repeats still short of this week's count, then what's not planned. It leaves breathing room and never goes past your daily capacity. One bar: **Accept plan** / **Not now**. Ticking a dashed block accepts that block only.
- **Needs a length** (inside the plan bar, only when there are some): "Buy stamps  10m 30m 1h". One tap and it joins the plan in the next fitting gap. This is the only question the morning asks.
- **Not planned** (one row under the day: "3 not planned"): only what has no day and didn't fit this week. Leftovers from earlier days come here too, and the planner picks them up again. No "from earlier", no red.
- Flexible repeats with a slot today show as blocks; their ring shows the week's count.
- Review in the evening; "Close the day" sends what's left back to Not planned for tomorrow's plan.

## The seven scenarios

1. **CSC 316, Tue and Thu 10:15–11:30.** "CSC 316 every tue and thu 10:15 for 1h15 #CSC316". Fixed repeat; the planner plans around it.
2. **Exam in 2 weeks, study for it.** "CSC 316 exam nov 23 9am" (fixed to-do). Then "Study for CSC 316 exam 6h due nov 22". The planner spreads sessions over the gaps before it, each saying "exam in 7 days". Missed sessions go back to Not planned and get replanned.
3. **Gym 3 times a week, feeding "lose 4 kg".** "Weigh 72 kg by mar 1" makes the goal; "What gets you there?" offers "Gym 45m 3 a week": accept. The planner gives Gym three slots a week. Log weight with -0.5 kg on the goal.
4. **Morning routine.** "Morning every day 7am", then add steps with lengths (brush teeth 5, meditate 10, shower 10). A block each morning; tick steps or "All done".
5. **Save $1000 by 31 Dec.** "Save $1000 by dec 31"; "What gets you there?" offers "Put aside $84 every Fri". That repeat lands on Fridays; ticking it logs +$84 on the goal.
6. **Mom's birthday, 14 Mar.** "Mom's birthday every year mar 14". Reminder 09:00 that day.
7. **Buy stamps.** Type "Buy stamps", tap **10m**. The planner drops it into a small gap today or tomorrow. If you skipped the chip, tomorrow's plan bar asks "Buy stamps 10m 30m 1h" once.

## Monday morning

You open Today. Classes and the morning routine are solid blocks; the gaps already hold a dashed plan: Gym 45 min at 16:00, exam study 60 min at 13:30, lab report 90 min at 19:00. The plan bar asks for one length ("Email TA  10m 30m 1h"): one tap. You tap Accept plan, or just tick the first block when you start.

## When the day slips

You missed the 13:30 study block. A quiet row: "1 block didn't happen. **Replan the rest**". One tap fits it, and anything after it that no longer fits, into later gaps or tomorrow, as a new dashed plan. Nothing moves without that tap.
