# Round 11: every card closes in one tap, and the system earns trust

Keeps round 10 (one card in four states; no limit maths on screen; two marks; Decide names its subject; Move → time). Changes:

1. **Every button is an outcome.** No button opens another question.
   - Free time: "Email TA fits now · **Start**". Start runs a timer; Done stops it. No "How long?", and nothing is guessed: the time you spent is what's recorded.
   - Check-in: **Done** · **Did 45m, rest → 12:45** · **Move → 12:45**. "Did part" shows the most likely split as a button: half of the block, with the rest placed in the next free slot. A small "other amount" link sits under the buttons.
   - Morning check-in: one compact list, each row with three small toggles (done · part · not yet), all set to "not yet" by default, and one button: **Rebuild the rest of today**.
2. **"Session 1 of 5"** is the name of a split block, so it can't read as a fifth of the work. Done on it says "Done with session 1".
3. **Pin here, not Keep here.** A block's sheet offers Pin here (Kriyan won't move it), so "Keep" only ever appears in a Decide choice.
4. **The same marks, everywhere.** A grey ticked box for done, an empty box for to do, a clock for attend. Classes you attended are greyed, never struck through. The week shows exactly what Today shows, Lunch included.
5. **One outline, one meaning: now.** A new block has a small "new" tag, no outline. Dashed means only an agent's suggestion.
6. **Sunday evening: the week behind and the week ahead.** The card (Next state) says: "This week: 19 of 21 blocks done, 2 moved, nothing late. Next week is planned: 17 h of work, exam on Mon 23." Buttons: **Looks good** · **Show me the week**.
7. **What Kriyan learned, as a suggestion.** On the same Sunday card when it's true: "Your exam prep sessions ran 15 min long 4 times. Plan them at 1 h 15 from now on?" **Yes** · **No, stay at 1 h**. Kriyan never changes a length without asking; this is the only way lengths learn.
8. **Decide says free hours.** "Problem set 6 (4 h) and Practice exam (2 h, optional) need 6 h. Thu and Fri have 4 h free."

A planner for one person (here: a university student). Today is the app; phone has three round buttons (Menu, Add, Week). Agents (Claude, ChatGPT) read and write the same plan over MCP and a CLI, through the same planner.

| **Decide** | Something won't fit | "ENG 331 essay (4 h, by Thu) fits if one exam prep moves from Wed to Sat." | Move prep to Sat · Keep Wed, work 1 h longer · See the times |

One card on Today and one live card on the lock screen; buttons visible without a long press. Reminders you set are the only other notifications.

## The kinds of thing

| Kind | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To-do** | Once. Optional: **by** a date, **at** a fixed time, a length, **prep** hours, steps. With a date or prep, Kriyan plans it; without, it waits and the card offers it in a free stretch. | Buy stamps. Lab report, 3 h, by Wed. CSC 316 exam at Mon 23 Nov 9:00, prep 5 h. |
| **Repeat** | Again and again. **Fixed** (set days and time) or **flexible** (N a week; Kriyan finds slots). | CSC 316, Tue and Thu 10:15. Gym, 45 min, 3 a week. Morning, 7:00, with steps. |
| **Goal** | Where you're heading, by a date. Offers what feeds it. | Weigh 72 kg by 1 Mar. Save $1000 by 31 Dec. |

Holders: **Space** (coloured, nests) and a **note**.
