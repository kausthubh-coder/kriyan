kriyan

Plan the day calmly. Then do it.

Kriyan is Sanskrit for action. It turns everything you have to do into a plan for your time, and keeps that plan quiet enough to follow. Your AI can plan with you.

The mark

An ensō, the circle drawn in one breath. Here it is a day: one stroke that thickens through the hours you have planned and opens where you are now. It is drawn from two circles, the inner one shifted towards one o'clock, and the gap opens between one and half past two. Use it as it is: never rotate it, outline it, or fill it with a space colour.

Colour

Warm neutrals do almost all the work, like ink on dark paper. Surfaces get lighter as they come closer. There is no brand accent: the primary button is ink, and the only interface colour is vermilion, which means now, or late.

Type

Figtree, one family, three weights: 400 to read, 500 for labels, 600 for headings and the wordmark. Six sizes, each with one job. On phones body text grows to 16 and follows the system text size.

Icons

Lucide, one set, 1.75 stroke, 18px next to 14px text and 22px in the phone navigation. An icon always sits beside a word, except in a button with an accessible name.

Principles

Now firstEvery screen answers what now, what next, and does it fit, before anything else.
Quiet by defaultNeutrals do the work. Colour means one of your spaces, or the time.
No guiltLate work is one quiet line with a way out. Habits count weeks, not streaks.
InstantEvery action shows on the next frame and can be undone. Nothing waits on the network.
