# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Kriyan is documented for a technically capable owner who wants a personal productivity system they can inspect and operate across desktop, web, mobile, Convex Cloud, and one private VPS.

## Product Purpose

The public site explains Kriyan plainly, makes its data and trust boundaries legible, and gives an honest path from understanding the architecture to installing, operating, updating, and troubleshooting an owner-controlled deployment.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, capable, candid. The site should feel like a carefully annotated system map: calm enough to read, specific enough to operate, and explicit about what has and has not been verified.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI landing pages built from neon gradients, glass cards, and vague autonomy claims.
- Chat-first product framing that hides durable state, data authority, or operational responsibility.
- Infrastructure docs that blur tested source behavior, planned distribution, and live deployment proof.
- Dense terminal cosplay or editorial styling that makes practical guidance harder to scan.

## Design Principles

1. Explain the app to Convex to VPS reactive loop before introducing operational detail.
2. Show where each kind of data lives and which copy is authoritative.
3. Keep setup and recovery instructions scannable without concealing prerequisites.
4. Use status language that separates implemented, locally verified, planned, deployed, and released.
5. Make the same information usable on a phone at a server console and on a desktop during setup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and semantics. Support keyboard-only navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, zoom, phone and desktop layouts, and status communication that never relies on color alone.
