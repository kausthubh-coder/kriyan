The assistant acts, then shows Undo

Today agents can already add tasks directly, but time blocks they plan (propose_blocks) wait as proposals until you accept them. In the new model, everything happens immediately and lands in an activity log with Undo:

How you, automations, agents and the coach use it
  • You, in the app: type or say "plan my afternoon"; the change appears as a card with Undo.
  • Automations: Codex reads Moodle and Gmail at 07:00 through the CLI and adds tasks marked "from Moodle". Each run lists what it added, with Undo on each item.
  • Agents over MCP: Claude plans your week with the same operations and the same log.
  • Coach: one insight at a time, from your own history ("lab reports take you 1h 50m, not 1h 30m").
What this changes in the backend

Proposals become an activity log: every operation records who did it (you, an automation by name, or an agent), what it changed, and the data to undo it. Agent operations already go through one registry, packages/core/src/ops.ts, so the log and Undo live in one place. The in-app ask bar sends your words plus today's plan to a Claude model with those operations as tools, through a Convex action. That needs an API key, which is ask 2 above.

AGENTS.md defines "proposal" as a task that waits for a yes; that definition changes with this step.

iPhone as a PWA: what works

FeatureOn iPhone
Full-screen appYes. Since iOS 26, anything added to the Home Screen opens as a web app by default.
RemindersYes, through Web Push, once it's on the Home Screen and the person taps Allow (iOS 16.4 and later).
HapticsUnreliable. Safari has no vibration API; a hidden-switch trick worked on iOS 18, and a May 2026 report says a WebKit change may have broken it. Untested by me.
SoundsYes, through Web Audio after the first tap.
GesturesYes. Swipe, long press and double-tap work in the browser; edge-swipe back is the system's.
Install promptNo automatic prompt on iPhone, so onboarding shows the Share, then "Add to Home Screen" steps.
