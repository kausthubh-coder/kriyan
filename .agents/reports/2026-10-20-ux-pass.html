The five bugs, fixed
  • The now line on Upcoming floated over the header before 08:00. It now shows only inside the grid's hours.
  • Overlapping blocks halved each other ("Calculu…"). A block that starts 30 min or more later now sits on top, indented, so both titles show.
  • The week showed two all-day items per day, and anytime tasks only as a count. Now it shows up to 3 tickable rows, then "N more".
  • The phone add field hid the start of the sentence. It wraps to 3 lines now.
  • Phone heatmaps sat off to the right. They fit the width now, from the text edge, with month labels.
  • Found on the way: rows never showed a hover tint, because RN-web ends a Pressable's hover over its title. Rows track hover themselves now.
