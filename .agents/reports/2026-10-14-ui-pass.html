Kept on purpose: 6 taste looks and why
  • Phone add button leads (4.2×). Material 3 wants one solid add button; surface-colour buttons are deprecated.
  • Upcoming has no single lead (1.03×). On a week grid the plan itself is the content.
  • Agenda dates 4 to 7px apart. Each date follows a weekday name of a different length.
  • Today near misses at 273 and 20px. These are invisible hit areas; the visible rings sit on the text edge.
  • "100% bold" on phone Inbox and Browse. The app bar title's box is stretched to 322px. The measured weights are 400 and 500.
  • Progress buttons 6px apart at 390. Right-aligned buttons of different widths.

Research behind the layout: 3 findings

  • Notion Calendar uses roughly 9% hairlines for hours (write-up).
